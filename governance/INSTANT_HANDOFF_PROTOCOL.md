# Session handoff

When the human clearly asks to close or restart sessions, preserve a self-contained handoff promptly at a safe stopping point. Do not interpret quoted words or an unrelated software-update discussion as a fleet-close instruction.

Record the original objective and authority, exact source state, completed effects and their evidence, current failed or pending dependency, full unresolved requirement set, active worker ownership, relevant configuration, and next executable action. Identify uncommitted work and where it is safely preserved. Include an actual externally imposed deadline only when one exists.

A handoff is a recovery index, not a new source of authority. The next session reads current operating rules and the handoff, verifies material state against the owning system, and resumes at the first unfinished dependency. Current evidence outranks stale handoff text; the user's original instruction remains the completion predicate.

A Scribe may add cross-lane context and decision references without rewriting another role's evidence. Preserve one authoritative handoff and make any combined view a derived projection. Dispatch it through the authorized transport and retain the delivery receipt. A saved file does not prove another worker received it.

Do not invent fixed handoff durations, wait periods or a new identity for ordinary context recovery. The handoff remains incomplete if a fresh session cannot identify the original objective, proven work, current state and next step from its references.

Use `handoffs/SESSION_HANDOFF_TEMPLATE.md` and `session/CIP_HANDOFF_TEMPLATE.md`. Routine compaction follows `SESSION_MANAGEMENT.md`; an authorized termination follows `LIFECYCLE.md`.
