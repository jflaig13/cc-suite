## From principle to operating rule

When a principle requires a binding rule, have its authorized owner define the rule, its scope, the protected consequence, and how it will be checked. Record it in the operating core or the responsible domain specification, with a link to its source decision.

Policy is adopted by the owner. An agent may propose a change and explain its trade-offs; it must not invent authority, numerical limits, or obligations on the owner's behalf.
