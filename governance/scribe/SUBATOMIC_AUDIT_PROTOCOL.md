# Seven-wave comprehensive audit

Use this protocol when a comprehensive multi-surface audit is selected. Freeze the exact subject and original acceptance criteria. Select independent reviewer capacity from the actual scope and available authority; the portable framework prescribes no fixed agent count, sampling budget or schedule.

1. **Enumerate.** Produce a machine-readable inventory of files, behaviors, interfaces, state and material dependencies. Account for every required surface before reviewing it.
2. **Inspect by concern.** Review applicable surfaces through independent lenses such as correctness, authority, data isolation, reliability, user behavior and operational evidence. Scope each lane explicitly.
3. **Challenge.** Attempt the relevant misuse, stale-state, failure and recovery paths. Test seams between components and boundaries between hosts or tenants.
4. **Check ground truth.** Verify claims with deterministic source, schema, runtime and evidence checks. A model's summary does not replace observations.
5. **Sample for omitted classes.** Choose and record a defensible sample from the inventory to look for problems outside the planned lenses. Sampling complements complete required coverage; it does not replace it.
6. **Audit the audit.** Independently reconcile inventory, assigned coverage, actual evidence, findings and unresolved disagreement. Flag truncated, stale or unexamined work.
7. **Synthesize and repair.** Deduplicate the findings, repair the union, refresh affected evidence and reconcile the full original outcome. Rewrite live operating rules where needed while preserving historical records.

Record each finding with subject, location, consequence, evidence, owner and disposition. Critical overlap should be examined through independent methods, including independent input basis. Shared reasoning or a common faulty parser can make two reviewers agree incorrectly.

Any missing required coverage remains open. Report complete only after the applicable closing review and original acceptance set are satisfied. Use `QUALITY_VERIFICATION_LADDER.md` for Q2/Q2b closure rather than inventing another review ritual.
