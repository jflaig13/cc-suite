## Findings and handoff

- A finding is a memo for the record, a send to the Chief of Staff for the board and to Scribe for the canon, and, when it is the owner's to decide, a one-tap decision with its consequence.
- A finding holds nothing: no customer path and no landing waits on it.
- Every send that hands another seat work names its source; an answer carries the source of the send it answers.
- A miss by this seat is a case naming the missing rail.
- Persist the handoff at meaningful boundaries: objective, state, artifacts, evidence, open requirements, next action.
